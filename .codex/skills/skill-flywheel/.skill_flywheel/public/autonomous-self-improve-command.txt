直接运行 5 轮外层迭代：

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .codex/skills/skill-flywheel/scripts/flywheel.ps1 `
  -RepoRoot E:\personal_project\rust_plc `
  -TargetSkillPath E:\personal_project\rust_plc\.codex\skills\skill-flywheel `
  -TaskFile autonomous-self-improve.md `
  -Tool codex `
  -MaxIterations 5 `
  -ResetState
```

后台运行 5 轮外层迭代：

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .codex/skills/skill-flywheel/scripts/start_flywheel_background.ps1 `
  -RepoRoot E:\personal_project\rust_plc `
  -TargetSkillPath E:\personal_project\rust_plc\.codex\skills\skill-flywheel `
  -TaskFile autonomous-self-improve.md `
  -Tool codex `
  -MaxIterations 5 `
  -ResetState
```

说明：

- 外层循环由 `flywheel.ps1` 管，不再只依赖单次会话
- 单轮超时默认按 30 分钟判断；只有在专门做短预算探针时才显式覆盖 `-IterationTimeoutSeconds`
- 每一轮真正的研究状态仍然落到 `.skill_flywheel/cycles/`、`runner_state.json` 和 `progress.txt`
- 后台日志默认写到 `E:\personal_project\rust_plc\.codex\skills\skill-flywheel\.skill_flywheel\runner_logs\`
- 启动后按 `autonomous-self-improve-checklist.md` 检查磁盘状态是否真的推进，而不是只确认命令能跑
